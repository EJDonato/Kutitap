Most motivation is ningas cogon.

It flares up fast, like a cogon grass fire, then dies just as quickly. New planner, new goals, new you… for about a week.

Kutitap is the opposite: a small light that keeps twinkling. Little steps, every day, until “someday” finally becomes done.

HOW IT WORKS

Set it up once. Wake up to a plan.

1

Tell it about your week

Type it like you'd say it: “I work 8 to 5, Monday to Friday, and leave the house by 6.” Add up to 3 goals for the year.

2

It plans your week and your day

Your goals and someday tasks get a real slot, around work, classes and habits. Your schedule is ready before you are.

3

Focus, check in, catch up

A small timer floats on your screen. After each block it asks “Did you finish?” and quietly reshuffles the rest of your day if you fall behind.
